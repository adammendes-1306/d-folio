MYPOCKET: Personal Planner and Money Manager

FYP Poster Source code Manual e-book

Project description

MYPOCKET is a mobile application designed to help users stay organised by combining essential functions such as daily planner, money manager and to-do list. It allows users to plan their schedules, track their finances and manage plans within a single app.

By bringing these functions together, MYPOCKET helps users improve their productivity and maintain better control over daily activities and finances.

Problem statement

  • Inefficiency of app fragmentation
  • Difficulty in managing daily ideas
  • Lack of financial awareness

Objectives

  • To build an integrated application that eliminates app fragmentation
  • To develop a functional daily planner application for users to organize plans and schedules
  • To encourage financial awareness by providing a financial tracking feature

Achievements and learnings

  • Achieved

    • MYPOCKET platform makes it easier by putting everything in one place. People can use MYPOCKET platform to manage their life without getting frustrated. MYPOCKET platform is very useful, for managing events and tasks and wallet management.

    By recording daily transactions, users gain a clearer understanding of their financial patterns over time. To further enhance this awareness, a visualization summary was implemented using a pie chart, allowing users to interpret their financial data intuitively than through text alone

  • What you learned

    • Utilized modern web technologies to create a responsive and user-friendly interface.

Industrial Training Mini Project - Training Module Revamp

Website Demo Link Source code

Project description

Revamped the existing training module in the Entomo Portal by introducing a new training workflow with enhanced flexibility and functionality. The updated module allows administrators to assign training to employees, track training completion, and publish training materials for employees to self-assign. Employees can also complete assessments after training sessions to evaluate their understanding and knowledge. These improvements aim to provide a more flexible and engaging learning experience while supporting better training management and learning outcomes.

Problem statement

  • Time-consuming onboarding process
  • Difficulty tracking training progress
  • No centralized storage for training materials

Objectives

  • To streamline the onboarding process through a more efficient training workflow
  • To improve the monitoring of employee training progress and completion
  • To centralize training materials in one accessible platform
